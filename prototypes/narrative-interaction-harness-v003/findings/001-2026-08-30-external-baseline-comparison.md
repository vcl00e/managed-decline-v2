# Harness v003 external baseline comparison — acceptable shell, incomplete v003 replacement

**Date:** 2026-08-30
**Status:** accepted as a recovered player-facing shell / regression baseline, not a feature-complete upgrade to narrative-interaction-lab-v003

## External comparison

The player compared the harness v003 golden control directly against the original narrative-interaction-lab-v003.

Result:

- the recovered harness presentation felt **good enough**;
- however, many of the details present in the original v003 were missing;
- therefore the golden control did **not** feel like a complete upgrade or replacement for v003.

## Interpretation

This is consistent with the intended scope of harness v003 but needs to be explicit in project history.

Harness v003 recovers and protects:

- useful desktop scale;
- large focused VN presentation;
- persistent important context;
- sparse controls;
- reliable runtime/trace behavior;
- a non-chasing co-located control.

It does **not** inherit every authored detail, composition choice, environmental texture, narrative beat or presentation flourish that helped original v003 succeed.

Therefore:

> **Harness v003 is a stable presentation/runtime baseline, not the canonical content or detail baseline for every future prototype.**

Original v003 remains positive evidence for richer authored detail and entertainment value. Future scenario work should preserve or exceed relevant positive details rather than using the deliberately reduced golden control as permission to simplify them away.

## Movement observation

The player also observed that both the harness v003 control and v009 require essentially no movement.

That is acceptable for these local tests, but must not become a general consequence of the harness architecture. The original movement findings remain active: movement should matter when position changes participation, access, audience, intimacy, witnessing, responsibility or future possibility.

The harness should support stationary intimate scenes **and** movement-rich situations without privileging either as a universal default.
