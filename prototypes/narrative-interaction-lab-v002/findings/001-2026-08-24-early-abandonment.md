# Early abandonment: arrangement ecology did not create a compelling experience

**Date:** 2026-08-24  
**Prototype:** `narrative-interaction-lab-v002` arrangement-ecology revision  
**Status:** Strong negative prototype evidence

## Tester feedback

The tester stopped after approximately two minutes and reported:

> The play test is boring. It's not funny and there is no clear emotional journey. I spent 2 minutes on it and wanted to stop and never start again.

The tester then agreed with the diagnosis below and asked for the next prototype to be built from that correction.

## Interpretation

This is not a minor pacing or polish problem. The prototype failed before it meaningfully tested whether the arrangement ecology could sustain a 40-minute session.

The implementation was designed from the accepted causal architecture:

```text
situation
→ human arrangements
→ commitments / information / access
→ collision
→ renegotiation
→ residue
```

That architecture may still be useful as hidden narrative machinery, but the prototype mistakenly treated it as sufficient **content architecture**.

The opening supplied several reasonable things the player could become involved with, but did not first create a strong desire to continue. Its early material consisted largely of rain, messages, a council notice, venue restrictions, a bus timetable and people with defensible practical problems. The result had causal structure without a compelling player fantasy.

## Specific failures

### 1. No immediate hook

The opening deliberately began from a neutral free evening and offered three roughly equal attractors. In practice this produced indifference rather than self-authored desire.

**Correction:** desire still needs to be authored. Avoiding quest markers does not mean beginning neutrally and waiting for the player to manufacture interest.

### 2. Comedy was too weak

The slice contained occasional writerly quips, but little that was genuinely funny enough to carry the experience. Several lines explained that a situation was satirical rather than allowing the absurdity itself to land.

**Correction:** Managed Decline needs stronger comic premises, sharper character comedy and situations whose precise British absurdity is funny before narration labels them as satire.

### 3. No clear emotional journey

The first section mainly progressed:

```text
mild freedom
→ mild curiosity
→ mild concern
→ possible responsibility
```

That is causal progression, not an affective arc.

**Correction:** future slices must deliberately author emotional movement such as curiosity, delight, attraction/complicity, escalation, embarrassment/panic/tenderness, meaningful choice and payoff.

### 4. Characters were not magnetic enough

The cast was broadly sensible, articulate and defensible. Nobody in the opening generated a strong desire for another scene simply by being themselves.

**Correction:** test with one or two characters capable of carrying the experience through charisma, comic behaviour, attraction, conflict and surprise.

### 5. The arrangement model became the subject

The prototype foregrounded community-performance, publicity and evening-plan arrangements as though their collision would automatically become dramatic.

**Correction:** the arrangement ecology should answer **how compelling drama becomes interactive and consequential**, not **what drama is worth experiencing**. Content still requires authored comic premises, character fantasies, reversals, escalation, spectacle, mystery, romance and payoff.

### 6. The design became visible through the prose

Meta lines about the game not assigning tasks or labelling commitments exposed the design argument rather than disappearing behind the fiction.

**Correction:** remove designer voice. The player should be thinking about the character and situation, not congratulating the game for avoiding quest UI.

## Decision

Do **not** patch v002 into a longer or funnier version. Treat it as a hard failure of the current test construction.

The next conceptual prototype should be `narrative-interaction-lab-v003` and should be much shorter: approximately **8–12 minutes**.

Its first-order requirements are:

1. immediate hook within roughly 30–60 seconds;
2. genuine early comedy;
3. rapid desire to spend more time with a character;
4. a deliberate emotional journey with several meaningful changes in feeling;
5. a premise specific to contemporary Britain and Managed Decline;
6. clear player fantasy;
7. at least one choice that changes emotional/social meaning;
8. escalation;
9. satisfying payoff;
10. an ending that makes the player wish the slice continued.

The key success test is:

> **At minute two, does the player want minute three?**

and at the end:

> **Is the player disappointed that it stopped?**

The arrangement ecology may remain underneath the new slice, but it must not be what the slice is about.
