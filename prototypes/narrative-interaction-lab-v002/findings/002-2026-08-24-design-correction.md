# Design correction after early abandonment

**Date:** 2026-08-24  
**Status:** Accepted correction following v002 failure

The tester agreed with the interpretation that v002 had solved causal plumbing rather than entertainment.

The next prototype must not begin by asking whether the arrangement ecology works across a long session. It must begin by proving that a small piece of *Managed Decline* is worth continuing at all.

## New order of operations

1. Author a compelling player fantasy and emotional journey.
2. Give that journey a strong comic premise and magnetic character.
3. Ensure the player wants the next beat before asking them to reason about systems.
4. Add only enough hidden causal machinery to make choices matter and persist.
5. Expand duration only after the short slice creates pull.

## Immediate target

`narrative-interaction-lab-v003` should be approximately 8–12 minutes and should test:

- hook in the first 30–60 seconds;
- at least one genuine early laugh;
- rapid character curiosity/attraction/complicity;
- a deliberate emotional arc rather than flat accumulation of responsibility;
- escalating comic/dramatic pressure;
- one or more choices that materially change the social meaning of the scene;
- a satisfying ending with visible future pull.

The strongest success signal is not structural coverage. It is:

> The player reaches the end and is annoyed that there is no more yet.
