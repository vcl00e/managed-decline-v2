# Final conclusion — Narrative Interaction Lab v002

Date: 2026-08-25

Status: **Concluded — hard experiential failure, useful architectural finding**

## Evidence

The tester abandoned the arrangement-ecology prototype after roughly two minutes and reported that it was boring, not funny, had no clear emotional journey, and created no desire to continue or restart.

The failure occurred before the intended 40-minute arrangement-ecology questions could be meaningfully tested. Therefore v002 provides **no positive evidence that the arrangement ecology can sustain a long session**.

## What failed

The prototype treated the arrangement ecology as though it could generate compelling content by itself. It supplied:

- coherent causal state;
- several human arrangements;
- commitments and information;
- institutional pressure;
- map-level attention choice;
- convergence and residue.

But it did not first supply a sufficiently compelling player-facing experience:

- no strong immediate hook;
- weak comedy;
- insufficient character magnetism;
- no deliberate emotional journey;
- little compelling player fantasy;
- understated dramatic material that felt like design machinery rather than entertainment.

The opening also allowed design language and experimental intent to leak into the prose, further exposing the machinery.

## Accepted interpretation

The arrangement ecology is **not rejected**. Its role is corrected.

It can remain valuable as hidden dramatic infrastructure answering questions such as:

- what people are trying to make happen;
- what commitments, information, access and material conditions constrain them;
- how player conduct changes social and practical possibilities;
- how consequences persist as residue and new affordances.

It should **not** be treated as the entertainment proposition or as a sufficient content-generation method.

The corrected hierarchy is:

1. compelling human/satirical premise;
2. desire to spend time with particular people;
3. authored emotional journey and escalation;
4. meaningful player expression/participation;
5. perceptible character/world response;
6. hidden arrangement/social systems preserving and propagating consequences.

## Consequence for prototyping

The intended 40-minute test was stopped. The next experiment was deliberately reduced to an 8–12 minute slice (`narrative-interaction-lab-v003`) testing whether Managed Decline could first earn minute three through hook, comedy, character desire, emotional movement and future pull.

## Final status

**V002 is concluded as a failed experiential prototype.**

Its implementation and findings should remain in the repository because the failure materially changed the design direction. Do not use its story as accepted content and do not cite it as evidence that the arrangement ecology sustains long-form play.
