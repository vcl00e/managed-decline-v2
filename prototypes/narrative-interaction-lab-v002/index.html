Managed Decline · Narrative Interaction Lab v002

Listening Exercise

One Friday evening in Greystone

A community broadcast, a locked hall and a compact social map

This prototype tests whether a compact diorama and restrained interaction can sustain a 25–40 minute dialogue-first session without turning the evening into a process layer.

There are no announced objectives, exposed relationship values or optimisation meters. Read the situation, decide what matters and act as you would want this protagonist to act.

Three test conditions vary the dosage of narrative support. Use blind mode for formal comparison.
