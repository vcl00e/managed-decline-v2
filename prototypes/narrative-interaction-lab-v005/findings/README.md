## Carry-forward result

V005 provides directional evidence for retaining:

- a compact continuous lived space;
- visible autonomous NPC movement;
- independent events that can be heard or missed;
- physical social convergence;
- discoverable practical problems;
- and occasional **situated intervention** where the player physically helps because the live situation gives them a reason to.

It also establishes that the spatial layer must **not** demote the project's strongest dramatic channel. Important conversations should return to v003-level authored/VN focus rather than becoming NPC polling or small overhead dialogue.

The next target is:

> **A close, lived diorama containing moving autonomous people and events worth noticing; occasional concrete situated interventions that arise naturally from what people are trying to do; important conversations returning to full authored VN-level dramatic focus; contextual social interaction instead of NPC polling; and materially complicated situations whose underlying scarcity or conflict cannot be abolished simply by discovering the truth.**

## Important cautions

Do not treat the initial trace's dialogue-choice frequencies or relationship deltas as clean preference evidence; see finding 002.

Do not generalise the radio extension-reel interaction into a requirement for fetch quests or environmental puzzles. The useful finding is the causal shape of **discover problem → form intention → physically help → situation changes**.

Do not treat the community-hall outcome structure as production-ready political satire. The next scenario should preserve real material scarcity and competing legitimate needs rather than resolve the conflict through an easy conspiracy/reversal.
