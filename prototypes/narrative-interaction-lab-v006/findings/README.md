## Concise result

V006 produced positive evidence for both ends of the proposed hybrid:

- socially meaningful spatial embodiment can add something dialogue-only presentation would flatten;
- focused VN treatment increases immersion for important conversations;
- expressive semantic choices can define the player's stance even without large branching;
- large positioning/commitment choices such as **leave with Tabitha / stay at the flat** can produce especially legible agency.

However, the connective implementation failed:

- fixed global timestamps produced dead time and pacing anxiety;
- the player felt pressure to poll for content;
- NPCs could be visibly active while remaining socially inaccessible;
- and several mapped spaces lacked distinct narrative function.

The accepted correction is:

> **Keep continuous social geography and focused VN drama, but use elastic diegetic time and make live situations participable through meaningful positioning rather than NPC polling.**

That correction is tested in `../../narrative-interaction-lab-v006b/`.
