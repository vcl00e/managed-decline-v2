# Final conclusion — Narrative Interaction Lab v006b

Date: 2026-08-26

Status: **Concluded — successful targeted correction; proceed to v007**

## What v006b was trying to establish

V006b was not another broad test of whether explorable space or VN presentation are worthwhile independently.

V006 had already shown that spatial presence could add meaning, but its fixed invisible timeline produced dead time, anticipatory scrambling, inaccessible NPC activity and renewed polling pressure.

V006b therefore kept the same scenario and tested one narrow correction:

> **Can continuous geography, elastic diegetic time, live-situation participation and meaningful positioning make the map/VN synthesis feel like one narrative interaction system rather than traversal between scripted beats?**

## Overall result

**Yes, directionally and strongly enough to conclude this experiment.**

Across two completed runs, the tester reported:

- waiting for the game to continue: **Never**;
- pressure to poll people for refreshed content: **No**;
- meaningful decisions about where to be: **Yes**;
- ability to join or stay out of situations naturally: **Yes**;
- VN transitions: acceptable / fine;
- primary attention: **the people and situation**.

This directly reverses the main v006 failure signals.

More importantly, the player used movement differently across the two runs:

- one run deliberately stayed with Tabitha and physically avoided becoming part of Alex and Priya's viewing;
- the other deliberately joined the viewing, protected Tabitha's bedroom threshold for a moment, entered the focused room conversation, and eventually stayed at the flat rather than keep a prior station commitment.

The same map therefore supported different narrative conduct rather than merely different routes between dialogue nodes.

## Accepted design conclusions

### 1. The diorama's strongest gameplay function is social positioning

The clearest successful formulation is:

> **Position yourself in relation to an unfolding human situation.**

Useful spatial verbs include:

- approach;
- remain;
- withdraw;
- follow;
- accompany;
- intercept;
- join;
- stay out;
- cross or protect a threshold;
- be present;
- deliberately be elsewhere.

Movement is not valuable merely because the player controls an avatar.

It becomes narrative interaction when physical position changes:

- participation;
- audience;
- intimacy;
- responsibility;
- access;
- what can be witnessed;
- and what the player can still do next.

The strongest tester formulation was that movement and positioning had begun to turn from a game mechanic into a narrative choice.

### 2. Continuous geography does not require continuous uncompressed time

V006's fixed-rate master timeline is rejected.

V006b supports a more precise rule:

> **Keep time live while presence, observation, anticipation or possible action have meaning. Compress genuinely empty intervals rather than simulating seconds for their own sake.**

This is stronger than a blanket rule to keep all waits short.

In one run, the tester spent substantial real time moving around before initiating the first important conversation but still reported never feeling that they were waiting for the game. The important distinction is therefore **chosen inhabitation versus forced dead time**, not simply duration.

### 3. NPC activity should be socially permeable, not pollable

The v006 overcorrection made Alex and Priya animated scenery: visible but not naturally joinable.

V006b instead exposed the **situation** as the interaction unit:

- join the viewing;
- stay with Tabitha;
- hang back and listen;
- protect the bedroom threshold;
- let Alex handle it;
- join the important room conversation;
- or stay out.

This removed polling while restoring agency.

Working principle:

> **Do not expose NPCs as dialogue inventories. Expose meaningful ways to participate in, observe or avoid what people are currently doing.**

### 4. Focused VN presentation remains useful for high-bandwidth conversation

The tester reported that the transitions into focused VN presentation felt fine in both runs.

The working division is therefore retained:

**Lived-space layer**
- presence;
- movement;
- thresholds;
- joining and leaving;
- ambient exchange;
- observation;
- sparse physical action.

**Focused VN layer**
- high-semantic conversation;
- authored dramatic pacing;
- expressive stance choices;
- emotionally or strategically dense interaction.

The remaining design problem is no longer primarily how to alternate these modes. They can operate as different bandwidths of one causal experience.

### 5. Choices do not all need the same kind of consequence

The earlier v006 discussion clarified three useful levels:

1. **Self-definition** — what stance did the player choose?
2. **Fictional acknowledgement** — how did other people interpret it?
3. **Situation divergence** — what actually became different afterward?

Expressive dialogue choices can be worthwhile at levels 1–2 without manufacturing large branches.

Choices about presence, commitments, access, disclosure or irreversible action should often reach level 3 when causally warranted.

The final stay/leave decision remains a strong model because it is simultaneously:

- human-readable;
- spatial;
- social;
- commitment-bearing;
- and immediately consequential.

### 6. Non-intervention is valid play

The first v006b run strongly supports allowing the player to decide:

> **This is not my problem to take over. I am staying with the person I came for.**

The game should not imply that every surfaced situation is a quest demanding intervention.

However, non-intervention must not collapse into lack of experience.

If the player stays with somebody instead of joining a conflict, the game can reward that through:

- companionship;
- small conversation;
- observation;
- shared routine;
- helping with one meaningful thing;
- private context;
- humour;
- or simply time together.

Character company can itself be a reward.

### 7. Prefer compact interactive topology with rich environmental texture

The simplified v006b flat carried the story more clearly than the broader v006 layout.

The tester nevertheless noticed some loss of texture and sense of how the flat was actually lived in.

The production implication is:

> **Keep the mechanically relevant topology compact, but make the environment materially rich.**

A detailed home can contain possessions, clutter, photographs, old furniture, boxes, plants, cables, laundry and traces of personal history without requiring every room or object to become a gameplay node.

## The next bottleneck: character interpretation and accumulated consequence

V006b exposed a new weakness precisely because the interaction grammar stopped dominating attention.

In the second run, the player:

- came to support Tabitha;
- explicitly prioritised Priya's right to know;
- joined Alex and Priya's viewing;
- influenced how the bedroom threshold was handled;
- entered the important room conversation;
- and finally broke an existing promise to walk Tabitha to the station by staying at the flat.

The tester expected Tabitha to challenge, resent or otherwise interpret this pattern more strongly.

Instead, Tabitha remained too accommodating and the situation held together despite the tester deliberately pushing the limits.

This exposes the next design requirement:

> **Characters must interpret patterns of conduct across multiple actions and commitments, not merely react to the most recent dialogue option.**

A character response should be able to reason over:

- what the player originally came to do;
- promises already made;
- whose needs the player repeatedly prioritised;
- whether they inserted themselves into somebody else's problem;
- whether words and later actions were consistent;
- what they protected, exposed or ignored;
- and where they ultimately chose to be.

The desired output is not a visible relationship score. It is believable initiative, confrontation, gratitude, distance, reinterpretation, changed access and future behaviour.

## What remains unproven

V006b does **not** establish:

- campaign-scale exploration;
- whether a large explorable town justifies its production cost;
- several simultaneous arrangements across multiple locations;
- phone / asynchronous interaction as part of the same grammar;
- convincing long-term character interpretation;
- robust escalation and failure;
- campaign-scale residue;
- or production-quality environmental density.

Both runs answered **Unsure** when asked whether removing explorable space entirely would make the experience worse. This remains a real caveat. The experiment provides strong evidence for particular spatial interactions, not proof that unlimited exploration is intrinsically desirable.

## Final interaction stack carried forward

```text
COMPACT LIVED SPACE
people + thresholds + audience + movement
        ↓
LIVE SOCIAL SITUATION
something people are actually doing
        ↓
PLAYER POSITIONS THEMSELVES
join / remain / follow / withdraw /
accompany / intercept / stay elsewhere
        ↓
LIGHT SPATIAL + AMBIENT INTERACTION
        ↓ when semantic bandwidth is needed
FOCUSED VN CONVERSATION
        ↓
WORLD RESUMES
        ↓
CHARACTERS INTERPRET THE PATTERN OF CONDUCT
        ↓
ARRANGEMENTS / ACCESS / RELATIONSHIPS CHANGE
        ↓
NEW SITUATION
```

## Next experiment: v007

Do not make v006c merely to polish *The Viewing* further.

V007 should move to a larger slice and test whether the now-accepted interaction grammar survives **multiple connected human arrangements**.

Primary question:

> **Can several ordinary arrangements interact across a larger slice of life such that the player's presence, commitments, information and conduct accumulate into consequences that characters correctly interpret and that manufacture the next situations?**

A suitable v007 should introduce, at minimum:

- more than one human arrangement;
- an existing commitment;
- a new opportunity or request;
- at least one practical/material/institutional pressure;
- more than one meaningful place or channel;
- accumulated conduct that one or more characters must interpret;
- and persistent residue that changes what becomes possible next.

Carry the v006b map/VN grammar forward without reopening it unless the larger slice exposes a new failure.

## Final status

**V006b is concluded as a successful targeted correction.**

It removes the major v006 dead-time/polling failure and provides the strongest evidence so far that spatial positioning can carry narrative agency in *Managed Decline*.

The project should now advance to v007, where the central risk shifts from basic interaction presentation to **arrangement interaction, character interpretation and accumulated consequence**.
