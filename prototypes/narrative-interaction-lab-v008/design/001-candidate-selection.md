# V008 candidate selection

**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Status:** internal design gate completed before implementation

## Target question

> Can a short one-to-one Tabitha interaction recover the hook, comedy and desire-for-company demonstrated by v003 while using the accepted v006b lived-space/VN grammar and avoiding the monologue, empty-company and brittle-runtime failures of v007–v007d?

## Candidate A — another frontage investigation

Tabitha finds something concealed on a public building and the pair investigate it.

**Rejected.** It is too close to v007d. Even a better implementation would make the next result hard to interpret because the player would again be walking between clues in a small authored investigation.

## Candidate B — prepare the room together

Tabitha and the player set out chairs or repair a minor event setup problem.

**Rejected.** This provides physical activity but weak entertainment. It risks converting companionship into errands and would test whether busywork is tolerable rather than whether character interaction is magnetic.

## Candidate C — the old build

A forgotten kiosk in the community-hall lobby still contains an archived build of the council training programme that turned Tabitha into a public cautionary character. The pair discover an outtake and accidentally print a personalised resilience plan. They can amend the facilitator notes and decide where the physical sheet remains.

**Selected.**

It offers:

- an immediate satirical hook grounded in Tabitha's established dramatic territory;
- a playful object both people can operate;
- Tabitha initiative without turning her into a dialogue terminal;
- player control over the programme, the notes and the final physical residue;
- short contextual exchanges rather than biography;
- a modest emotional movement from mockery, through a prickly archived outtake, into collaborative authorship;
- several visibly different endings without a relationship score or mandatory future-date reward.

## Boundary

This is prototype-grade test material, not accepted production canon. V003 already established that the exact Community Compass causality was not accepted lore. V008 uses the premise as a controlled interaction test because it is immediately legible and character-specific; success would validate an interaction/content method, not canonise the kiosk or archived build.
