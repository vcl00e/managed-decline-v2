# V008 plain interaction script — The Old Build

This document was reviewed before implementation. It contains no state labels, relationship scores or intended emotional interpretation.

## Entry — live space

An old council kiosk wakes in the community-hall lobby. Tabitha leaves the entrance and walks over without waiting for the player.

**TABITHA:** No.

**TABITHA:** They kept the demo.

The player may join her, remain elsewhere or leave.

## Screen one

**KIOSK:** YOUR FRIEND HAS STARTED READING LOCAL HISTORY. WHAT SHOULD YOU DO?

Options:

1. Ask what she's reading.
2. Tell a member of staff.
3. Move her to Fiction.
4. [Give Tabitha the mouse.]

Possible immediate reactions:

- **TABITHA:** A rare point for speaking to the person.
- **TABITHA:** Congratulations. You work here now.
- **TABITHA:** Austen failed as intervention.
- Tabitha chooses the most escalatory answer.
- **TABITHA:** It congratulated me. Disgusting.

## Archived outtake

**RECORDED TABITHA:** I just think people should be allowed to ask questions.

**NARRATOR:** Statements like this may sound reasonable.

**TABITHA:** They kept that take.

Options:

1. [Replay it.]
2. You look furious.
3. You nearly laughed.
4. [Skip it.]

Possible reactions:

- **TABITHA:** No.
- **TABITHA:** They had hidden the biscuits. It was hour six.
- **TABITHA:** The director whispered “more susceptible” like it was a mood.
- **TABITHA:** Thank you.

Tabitha notices a tab marked FACILITATOR TOOLS.

**TABITHA:** I pressed it.

A printer starts behind them.

## Live space — printer

Tabitha heads toward the printer. The player can follow, leave or let the page fall. If they follow, they physically take the sheet from the tray.

## Printed plan

**PRINTER:** PERSONAL RESILIENCE PLAN: ATTEND AN APPROVED SOCIAL ACTIVITY WITH A TRUSTED PEER.

**TABITHA:** We are literally at the approved social activity.

The lower half says FACILITATOR NOTES.

The player chooses what to write:

1. ASK HER WHAT SHE IS READING.
2. BUY BISCUITS BEFORE HOUR SIX.
3. DO NOT WHISPER “MORE SUSCEPTIBLE”.
4. [Hand Tabitha the pen.]

Possible reactions:

- **TABITHA:** Look at that. Direct engagement survives contact with stationery.
- **TABITHA:** That would have changed the entire shoot.
- **TABITHA:** Finally, a transferable learning outcome.
- Tabitha writes: STOP MAKING TRAINING GAMES ABOUT PEOPLE WHO CAN READ.
- **TABITHA:** Concise.

## Live-space residue

Tabitha walks away from the machine and sits by the lobby bench. The player is holding the sheet.

The player can:

- pin it to the staff noticeboard;
- return it to the printer tray;
- give it to Tabitha;
- take it out of the building;
- or abandon the interaction earlier.

The final response changes according to the chosen note and some earlier conduct. No screen announces relationship progression.
