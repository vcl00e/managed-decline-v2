# V008 interaction and writing review

**Status:** passed for implementation with explicit remaining uncertainty

## Interaction-design review

### What does the player actually do?

- decides whether to join Tabitha at the kiosk;
- operates or yields the old programme;
- chooses how to respond to an archived outtake;
- follows the printer noise in live space;
- physically retrieves the printed sheet;
- authors or delegates a concrete amendment;
- carries the resulting object to a chosen destination.

This is sufficient activity for a short interaction. It is not merely `select supportive response`.

### What does Tabitha initiate?

- leaves the entrance and approaches the kiosk;
- recognises the old build;
- reacts differently to programme choices;
- identifies the outtake;
- opens facilitator tools without permission;
- moves to the printer;
- writes her own amendment when given the pen;
- moves away while the player decides what becomes of the sheet.

### Does earlier conduct affect later material?

Yes. The first programme answer and the outtake response alter later callbacks. The note changes the physical sheet and ending copy. The final spatial placement creates distinct residue.

### Is it worthwhile without progression labels?

Provisionally yes. The entertainment proposition is the pair privately operating and editing an absurd institutional artefact. There is no relationship score, route-completion announcement or future-date invitation carrying the scene.

## Writing review

### Removed or rejected

- explanations of Tabitha's public/private identity;
- “I'm interested in the real you” reassurance;
- choices that state the intended relationship interpretation;
- long speeches about what the old programme did to her;
- generic intimacy language;
- a future-plan reward used as proof of closeness.

### Retained

- terse Tabitha reactions;
- specific production memories (missing biscuits, “more susceptible” direction);
- humour arising from the exact interface and artefact;
- choices phrased as immediate actions, observations or text to write;
- one prickly beat without requiring Tabitha to explain it.

### Line-pressure check

No focused node contains more than three authored turns before player input. Choice responses are one or two short turns. Character information appears through use of the old build rather than a biography paragraph.

## Comparison with positive precedent

V003 succeeded because it had an immediate satirical premise, strong Tabitha company, comedy, an emotional turn, meaningful participation and visible acknowledgement. V008 retains those target qualities while changing the interaction form:

- private shared operation rather than attending a facilitated presentation;
- physical map transitions before, between and after focused exchanges;
- player authorship of an artefact rather than only choosing a stance;
- compact duration rather than a 13-decision narrative route.

## Remaining uncertainty

The following cannot be certified on paper:

- whether the programme-within-a-programme feels too interface-heavy;
- whether the jokes feel natural in sequence rather than individually;
- whether the final sheet placement produces enough interpersonal payoff;
- whether reusing Tabitha's training-programme territory feels fresh after v003;
- whether the interaction is genuinely enjoyable rather than merely well structured.

Those are appropriate internal whole-play and eventual external playtest questions.
