# V009 pre-implementation interaction / comprehension / writing review

**Status:** candidate passes paper review; implementation may begin

## First-pass comprehension

Cold-read summary:

> Tabitha finds an instant camera with two shots. One of you photographs the other, you can reverse who goes first, then the camera changes hands. You end with one or two instant prints and decide who keeps them.

No prior Managed Decline lore is required to understand the immediate activity.

### Terms the player must understand

- instant camera;
- two shots left;
- take a picture;
- print develops;
- trade / keep / leave the prints.

There is no institutional vocabulary required to follow the action.

### Stakes

Low-stakes and immediate: what picture gets taken, how each person behaves in front of the camera, and what happens to the physical photos.

The scene does not need the player to infer a political premise before the banter works.

## Reciprocal interaction review

The candidate passes the coupling test because causal control changes hands:

1. Tabitha initiates the activity.
2. Player may accept or reverse Tabitha's proposed order.
3. Subject behaviour changes photographer reaction.
4. Camera changes hands.
5. Player directly gives Tabitha a photographic direction.
6. Tabitha follows/responds to that direction.
7. Shared artefacts reflect both people's conduct.
8. Player determines their final allocation.

This is different from v008's `NPC moves → player catches up → trigger` sequence.

## Sticky companionship review

Tabitha stays in the same social area for the entire activity.

No authored beat is unlocked by reaching a new Tabitha waypoint.

Map movement remains available for leaving or repositioning, but continuation does not require tailing the NPC.

## Writing review

### Keep

- “Two left.” — immediately establishes the constraint.
- “Don’t do anything weird.” — ordinary setup that invites playful disobedience.
- “Christ. You look electable.” — short reaction to a visible player action.
- “Be normal about this.” — keeps control dynamic playful without explaining intimacy.
- “There. Public-sector safe.” — small Tabitha-specific institutional residue without requiring lore.

### Risks removed

The script deliberately avoids:

- speeches about Tabitha's public identity;
- phrases such as `the real you`, `I am interested in you`, `I understand you`;
- explaining why taking pictures is emotionally meaningful;
- multi-clause political satire;
- callbacks requiring memory of several prior concepts;
- choices that name the intended relationship stance.

### One line rejected during review

Rejected:

> “At least this time I get to choose the version of me people keep.”

Reason: it explicitly states the thematic interpretation and turns the activity back into writerly public-symbol exposition.

The prints should carry that possibility without the character explaining it.

## Interaction-noise review

The player is not asked repeatedly whether they want to remain with Tabitha.

There is only one meaningful continuation decision after the first picture: use the remaining shot or stop. The stakes have changed because a real first print now exists and only one exposure remains.

## Presentation review

V009 must import the recovered harness v003 player-facing shell unchanged.

Blocked changes:

- shrinking the shell/VN;
- bottom-aligning focused VN;
- timed expiry for important text;
- new dashboard/log panels;
- putting scene comprehension into small ambient overlays.

## Paper verdict

The candidate is sufficiently clear and reciprocal to implement.

This is **not** a judgment that it will be fun. Implementation must preserve the simple script rather than adding lore or intermediate mechanics, and rendered self-play must still decide whether it earns external testing.
