# V010b corrective journey script

This keeps v010's hall → route → optional stop → station structure and changes only how the journey feels between those points.

## Departure

The player and Tabitha are already leaving together.

**Tabitha:** “Which way? I’m not emotionally attached to either pavement.”

The player chooses by walking.

Tabitha walks in formation with the player rather than trailing to their last position.

## High street

The pair emerge side-by-side onto the brighter high street.

### Beat A — bus shelter pinch

A bus has just emptied out. People briefly fill the pavement around the shelter.

The pair naturally compress into single file to get through, then resume side-by-side afterwards.

No prompt.

Bottom-middle narration briefly acknowledges the shared adjustment:

> The pavement pinches around the bus shelter. You fall into single file for a few steps, then Tabitha comes back alongside you.

### Beat B — bus display micro-action

A bus display flickers between `12 MIN`, `DUE`, and `12 MIN` again.

If the player comes close, one optional prompt appears:

**E — Look at the bus display with Tabitha**

If used:

**Tabitha:** “Good. Time is circular.”

If ignored, nothing is punished and the walk continues.

### Beat C — shop suggestion

Tabitha notices the late corner shop.

**Tabitha:** “I could eat something. Your call.”

The player can turn into the shop as in v010 or simply continue.

If the player continues far enough for the shop to fall behind, the live narration updates without requiring another action:

**Tabitha:** “No crisps. Severe administration.”

She remains alongside the player. No menu opens.

### Beat D — after the shop

A bus pulls away ahead and leaves the high street briefly quieter.

**Tabitha:** “There. We survived commerce.”

If the shop was used, the line reflects what actually happened instead.

## Quiet cut-through

The pair enter the quieter route side-by-side.

### Beat A — railings pinch

A narrow gap between railings forces one of them behind the other for several steps.

The formation compresses automatically, then expands once the path opens.

No prompt.

### Beat B — small observation

A lit upstairs window reveals somebody doing an elaborate exercise routine in a tiny room.

**Tabitha:** “Strong commitment to being visible.”

No choice is required.

### Beat C — park suggestion

Tabitha nods toward the pocket park.

**Tabitha:** “Five minutes? Or not.”

Turning into the park accepts. Continuing declines.

If ignored:

She glances back once when the park is behind you.

**Tabitha:** “No five minutes. Relentless forward motion.”

### Beat D — fox micro-action

An urban fox appears ahead near the final stretch.

The pair both slow slightly.

Optional prompt:

**E — Stop for the fox**

If used, both stop together for a moment.

**Tabitha:** “We interrupted a meeting.”

If ignored, the fox slips away and the walk continues.

## Station

The routes converge exactly as in v010.

The player can lead the pair into the station or peel off at the forecourt.

If the player peels off, the formation ends explicitly. Only then does Tabitha receive an independent destination and walk away.

## Interaction-density rule

The journey should not feel like:

`walk → E prompt → walk → E prompt`.

The intended rhythm is:

`movement → observation → formation change → optional micro-action → movement → suggestion → spatial response → acknowledgement → movement → threshold`.

Most beats happen because the pair move through a place together. Only a minority require explicit interaction.
